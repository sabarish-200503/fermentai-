What it does

Everything you need at the bench

Built for bioprocess engineers, researchers, and students working with fermentation and bioreactors.

Parameter optimization

Input your temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, and agitation and get organism-specific AI recommendations instantly.

Fault diagnosis

Describe symptoms like foam formation, DO crash, or stalled growth and get root cause analysis with corrective steps.

Real-time status badges

Live indicators show whether your parameters are in the optimal range — green, amber, or red at a glance.

Multi-organism support

Covers E. coli, S. cerevisiae, Lactobacillus, Aspergillus niger, CHO cells, and Bacillus subtilis.

Instant AI answers

Expert-level guidance in seconds rather than searching through textbooks or papers.

Built for research

Select your organism

03

Get AI recommendations
